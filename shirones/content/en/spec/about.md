# About

Hi, I'm **Dany** 👋

**Dany's Lab** is my personal laboratory: a portfolio, a blog and a coffee recipe
notebook in one place. I document my projects, my experiments and what I learn
along the way.

- **The blog** — notes and lessons learned;
- **The coffee lab** — precise, reproducible drink recipes;
- **The projects** — what I am working on.
