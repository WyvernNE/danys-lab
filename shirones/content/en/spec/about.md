# About

Hi, I'm **Dany** 👋

I like comfort. Not luxury: everyday life that just flows, the drink that hits
the spot, the commute that takes ten minutes less. So I test, I score and I
tweak. This site is my logbook, from Bern.

## Where I come from

- **Between the bar and the keyboard**: bartender on one side, IT person on the
  other (BSc in Computer Science, HE-Arc).
- **Community & Growth**: several years growing the SwissBorg community, in Web3.
- **Events and marketing**: projects run for others, people brought together
  around a good idea.

## How it works here

Every post is a **numbered experiment**: a hypothesis, a test, then a **verdict**
with a **Comfort Index** out of 10. Everything is sorted into four pillars:

- **Drink** — coffee, matcha, cocktails;
- **Move** — sport, outings and [events](/danys-lab/en/events/);
- **Bern** — walks, spots and addresses;
- **Build** — projects, case studies and the backstage of this site.

All the numbers live on the [dashboard](/danys-lab/en/dashboard/). Want to work
together? Head [this way](/danys-lab/en/collab/).
