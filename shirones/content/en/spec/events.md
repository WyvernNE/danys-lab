## The idea

A bit of sport, a good drink, nice people. No stopwatch, no performance: the
point is to have a good time and discover a corner of Bern.

- **Move** — an outing for every level;
- **Drink** — we finish at a spot picked for the occasion;
- **Meet** — expats, locals, the curious: everyone is welcome.

Each event then becomes an experiment on the site, with photos, route and
verdict.
