## What I offer

I test things to make everyday life more comfortable, and I show the results,
numbers included. A partnership follows the same rule:
**honest, measured, useful**.

- **Sponsored experiment** — your product or venue tested in a lab experiment,
  with its Comfort Index;
- **Event** — a Move + Drink event hosted or supported by your brand;
- **Spot of the month** — your address featured in the Bern pillar;
- **Marketing project** — visibility, content or community for your business.

## My rules

- Any sponsored content is **clearly labelled** as a paid partnership.
- The verdict stays **mine**: I publish what I actually found.
- After each partnership, you get **the numbers**: views, clicks, attendees.

## The audience

Expats and locals in Bern who love good addresses, low-pressure sport and things
done well. Detailed numbers are available on request.
