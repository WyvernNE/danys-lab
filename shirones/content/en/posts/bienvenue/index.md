Welcome to **Dany's Lab**, the logbook of a Romand lost in Bern.

The idea: find my landmarks, one experiment at a time. I test, I score, I tweak,
and I publish the results.

## How to read this site

Every post is a **numbered experiment**. At the top: the hypothesis. At the
bottom: the **verdict**, with a **Comfort Index** out of 10, what I keep or drop,
and **+1 landmark** on the counter.

Four pillars:

- **Drink** — coffee, matcha, cocktails, in the [drinks lab](/danys-lab/en/series/labo-cafe/);
- **Move** — sport, outings and [events](/danys-lab/en/events/);
- **Bern** — walks, spots and addresses;
- **Build** — [projects](/danys-lab/en/projects/), case studies and the backstage of this site.

The numbers, and my current level, live on the [dashboard](/danys-lab/en/dashboard/).

*A little less lost than yesterday.*
